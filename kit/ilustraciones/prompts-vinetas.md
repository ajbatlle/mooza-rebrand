# Prompts para las tres viñetas horizontales

Son ilustraciones opcionales para rellenar un espacio vacío, en una franja horizontal a sangre. No son musas: dicen otra cosa (cuándo, función, público). Mismo estilo de las musas, más sutiles: menos negro sólido, más línea y mucho blanco.

Se usan en una sola conversación de ChatGPT. Primero el mensaje de estilo y después un mensaje por viñeta. ChatGPT no entrega 4:1; se pide 3:2 con el friso en el tercio del medio y se recorta. Si aparece color o gris, pedir: "Redraw it in pure black ink on a pure white background, no grey, no color."

## Mensaje 1: estilo

I'm going to ask you for three horizontal friezes, all in exactly the same style. Don't generate anything yet; just reply "ready".

Style: an early 20th-century hand-inked illustration, like a vintage printed card drawn by an expressive, theatrical illustrator. Confident, slightly uneven pen-and-ink contour lines of varying weight, a little short parallel hatching for shadow, very few areas of solid black, no gradients, no soft shading, no realism. Light and airy: more line than fill, lots of white space inside and around the drawing. Stylized, slightly elongated shapes with a gentle naive charm, the way a woodblock print or an Arts-and-Crafts illustration would draw them, with small decorative patterns (dots, stars, small leaves).

Technical rules for every image: black ink only on a pure white background, high contrast, no color, no grey tones, no paper texture, no border or frame, no text, no signature, no ground line, no cast shadow. Landscape format 3:2. The drawing is a long horizontal frieze that occupies only the middle third of the image height, with generous empty white space above and below. The frieze runs off both the left and the right edge of the image at the same height, so it can be cropped at any width.

## Mensaje 2: la cinta (cuándo)

Frieze 1: the ribbon. A single long, thin ribbon of fabric that enters from the left edge and leaves from the right edge in a slow wave, like a scarf floating in the air. Along its path it carries small drawn objects tied to it or caught in its folds: a sun with short rays, a crescent moon, and a few small stars, in that order from left to right (day turning into night). The ribbon has a few small dots and leaves as pattern. No figures, no hands. Same style and technical rules as before.

## Mensaje 3: el telón (comienza la función)

Frieze 2: the curtain. A theater curtain valance seen from the front: a row of soft draped swags hanging in even curves from small rosettes, with long thin tassels between them. The folds are drawn with a few long curved lines and a little hatching. No stage, no seats, no people. Same style and technical rules as before.

## Mensaje 4: el público (ir con otros)

Frieze 3: the audience. A row of about ten spectators seen from behind, seated side by side, drawn only from the shoulders up: simple rounded heads and hair, a hat, a pair of glasses seen from the back, two or three of them leaning toward each other, one with a raised hand. Hair and shoulders with a little hatching, no faces, no stage, no seats drawn. The row runs off both edges of the image. Same style and technical rules as before.

## Después de generarlas

Las imágenes se convierten a tinta azul con transparencia, igual que las musas (ver `README.md` de esta carpeta), y se recortan a la franja: 7 columnas de ancho por 2 filas de alto en 4:5, unos 4:1.
